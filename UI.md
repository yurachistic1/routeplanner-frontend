# Buttons and controls

The app's design system for everything you tap, read or type into: **Tonal**, chosen on 2026-11-14 from three options (mockups: `designs/buttons.html`, git-ignored; the reasons are in `ROADMAP.md` §11). Everything you can tap has a fill. Lime is the main step. Selected things go lime (no ticks: they cost space and the lime edge or fill already shows it, 2026-11-15). There are no outlines and no arrows in buttons. All styles are in `css/styles.css`; colours are tokens in its `:root`.

**This file is the rulebook for every addition to the UI** (the user, 2026-11-14: surfaces must stay readable, usable and consistent, and reuse the existing designs in a way that makes sense). Read it before adding or changing any control, panel or text. Keep it up to date: a new kind of control is written up here as part of the same change.

## Principles

1. **Readable.** Text meets the contrast and size rules below on the surface it actually sits on (panel, card, map). Nothing to read sits straight on the map without a panel behind it.
2. **Usable.** Targets of 44px or more, every state visible (pressed, focus, disabled, busy, selected), and it works by touch, mouse and keyboard. Phones come first; check laptops too.
3. **Consistent.** The same job looks and behaves the same everywhere. A new control is built from the existing roles and tokens, not given its own colours, shapes or type.
4. **Reuse what makes sense.** Pick the existing role whose meaning fits (a choice of a few is chips, an action is a button, on/off is a toggle). If nothing fits, extend the system with a new pattern written up here, made of the same tokens, rather than a one-off. When it's unclear which pattern fits, ask the user before building.

## Adding something new: checklist

- Which role or pattern is it? Use its class (`.btn-*`, `.icon-btn`, `.chip`, card) rather than new CSS. A pattern from "Not built yet" below gets built as written there.
- Colours only from the `:root` tokens; no new hex values in components. Lime is for the main step and for selected or on; pink only for favourites and the logo's dot; amber only for warnings; red only for danger (and, faint, on the map for ways no one may walk).
- Sentence case, no arrows, one primary per panel.
- Text: 15px for body and notes, 13px the smallest anything readable gets, `--dim` for secondary text, never the old `#6c7889`.
- States: rest, pressed, focus-visible, disabled, and selected or busy where they apply; hover only inside `@media (hover: hover)`.
- Layout: fits a 360px-wide phone without cutting text (wrap to two lines rather than cut), and leaves no odd gaps on laptops.
- Check it in Chrome at phone size (`designs/phone.html`) and laptop size before calling it done, and add it to "Every control" below.

## Not built yet: how they should look

Specs for controls the app will likely need, so they arrive already consistent. Build them as written; change this section first if something has to differ.

- **Slider** (built 2026-11-16 as `.slider` in `.slider-field`, first used in GPS art's Custom sheet) (a range, e.g. hilliness, distance tolerance): a 6px track in `--tone`, the part up to the thumb lime; a 28px white thumb (`--text`) with a soft shadow, inside a 44px-tall touch area. The current value sits as text above the right end, 15px semibold, in `--text` (units in `--dim`). Label above the left end in `--dim`, 14px semibold, sentence case. Pressed: the thumb grows to 32px. Focus: the 3px lime outline round the thumb. Disabled: track `#1a2029`, thumb `#566070`. Arrow keys step it. If there are only a few fixed values, use chips instead.
- **Switch** (built 2026-11-20 as `.switch-row` holding `.switch`, `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`; first used for A to B's Minimise climb) (on/off for a setting): a 52×32 pill in `--tone` with a 24px white knob; on, the pill turns lime and the knob dark (`#0b1004`) slides right. The label sits to its left in `--text` (15px), with any explanation below in `--dim`. The whole row is the target, at least 44px tall. Pressed: the pill shrinks to 96%. Hover (laptops): the pill lightens. Disabled: pill `#1a2029`, knob and label `#566070`. For an action that toggles (favourite, satellite), keep using the toggle icon buttons instead.
- **Segmented control** (2–4 views of one thing, e.g. Map / Satellite / Hills): a `--card` pill holding equal segments; the chosen one is a lime pill with dark text (no tick needed, the shape shows it). More than 4, or choices that aren't views: chips.
- **Checkbox and radio** (in forms): 24px, `--tone` fill; checked is lime with the dark tick (`--tick`), or a dark dot for radio. The label is the target too.
- **Field with label, hint and error**: label above in `--dim` 14px semibold, the field as in Roles, hint below in `--dim` 14px; an error replaces the hint in `#ffd3e4` with a 2px `#ff6a5f` edge on the field. Say what's wrong and how to fix it.
- **Progress** (a long job with steps): a 6px `--tone` track filling lime, with a sentence in `--dim` saying what's happening. A short wait inside a button uses the button's spinner instead.
- **Grip** (built 2026-11-16, `.deck-grip`, GPS art on phones; A to B too since 2026-11-20; the behaviour is shared in `js/panel.js`): a 40×5 pill in `--dim-2` on the panel's top edge, inside a full-width 44px-tall target. Focus: the lime outline round the pill. `aria-expanded` says which. Red here means "no" as it does for danger. Via: a 13px dark bead (`#0b1004`) ringed 2.5px white. Finish: a 19px chequered disc (dark and white) ringed white. A closed route shows only the start where it closes; passing the start again keeps it the start. Favourites keep no via points, so only the finish shows; on their chart, the finish dot as in A to B.
- **Passes over the same street** (built 2026-11-20, `js/lanes.js`, A to B; reworked the same evening after ten laps looked knotted): the route is one line that moves sideways into its own lane where a stretch is run more than once, easing over at least 12px (and three times the sideways distance), so every lap runs on into the next without breaks. Each way keeps to its own left, as on British roads, earliest pass outermost, so passes going the same way stay together and shift across as a group at junctions instead of crossing. Zoomed out the passes bunch into one thick line, drawn once (at most 3 line widths, so 10 laps look much like 100; at zoom 13 and out all lanes span at most one line width, doubling each zoom in). From zoom 19.5 they spread out as thin 2.5px lines up to 2.5 line widths apart (about 0.7 m on the ground), so a bundle stays narrower than the street; up to about 100 passes fit (2026-11-23, the user: laps apart aren't needed sooner; it was 3.5 m apart from zoom 16, which made 4 laps there and back 25 m wide and knotted at every bend, then briefly 1.5 m from 17). They stay nearly solid (85%) where single lines fade. From zoom 19.5 the route is also no longer smoothed: single lines and bands follow the path (smoothing is for the whole-route view); a waypoint's corner is never rounded, so the line always reaches it. Turns (reworked 2026-11-23 after lanes broke into hooks, arrowheads and crossings at turns, junctions and waypoints): every lane turns about the same centre, like lines on a transit map. A bend is rounded wide enough for all its lanes, the inside one keeping a 6px radius; at a turn sharper than 115° the lanes all move out to its outside, so the inside one runs through the corner and the rest wrap round it in arcs (no rounding fits lanes inside a sharp corner without crossing or turning well short of it); turning straight back (a turnaround) they go round its far side, nesting like a hairpin. Anything the sideways move still folds over (a stretch too short for its lanes) is cut out. Freehand legs are drawn like any other; uncertain paths are dashed in their lane.
- **Messages**: a problem is the pink toast. Every routing error goes there (the user, 2026-11-23), never into a line in the panel, which would grow it and move controls from under the finger. In A to B: a change of routing that couldn't re-route the last leg ("Shortest can't route the last leg: its point is off the paths. It stays as it was.") and a tap that placed no point, in one sentence of what and why: too far ("That point is 23 km from the last one… legs go up to 20 km…"), nothing to route on where it landed, saying what the map draws there when it can (js/spot.js, from the map's own tiles: "Lewes Road (A22) is a trunk road. Routing leaves trunk roads out…", motorway, main road with no pavement, private, closed to walkers, "That's the River Thames…"; otherwise "Can't route to that spot: …" / "Can't start there: …"), "No paths near the last point", "No path goes nearer", "No route found", "No map data here" or "Couldn't route that leg" (no answer), each saying what to do (often: use Freehand). Long messages stay up longer (50 ms a character, 4.5-9 s); something undoable is the `#undo` bar with a tertiary action. On touch screens both can be swiped away (2026-11-21, the user; `swipeAway` in js/ui.js): sideways, or towards the edge they sit at (the toast up, the Undo bar down); they follow the finger, fading, and go past a third of their width or with a flick, else spring back; while held their timer waits (2 s more after springing back, 2.5 s for Undo); swiping away the Undo bar lets the removal stand; a tap on Undo is still a tap. The toast takes touches while it shows, on touch screens only (elsewhere taps go through it to the map). Mice and pens can't swipe them. Badges or counts: `--ltone` with `--lime-ink` text, at least 12px.

## Rules

- **One primary per panel.** If two buttons both feel primary, one of them is secondary.
- **Sentence case** everywhere ("Export GPX", "New run"). No spaced-out capitals on controls.
- **No arrows** in buttons. Previous and next use chevrons. Icons that mean something (heart, close, search, pencil) are fine, alone or next to words.
- **Text contrast:** main text `--text` (15.8:1 on the panel), secondary text `--dim` `#aeb8c6` (9.2:1) for notes, labels and hints, small print `--dim-2` (6.1:1). Nothing people need to read is under 13px; notes are 15px.
- **Every control has** a pressed look (shrinks to 96% and darkens), a keyboard focus look (3px lime outline, 3px out), and a disabled look (`#1a2029` fill, `#566070` text). Hover (laptops only) lightens the fill. Disabled is the only thing that looks faded.
- **Targets** are at least 44px; most are 48–52px.
- **Laptops:** the heart sits at the end of the Export row (‹ Export GPX › ♥, or Export GPX ♥ in GPS art), with New run / New search on a row below; favourites have just the one row. Phones keep the heart beside All routes and New run.
- **Mode shelf:** every mode looks the same (grey icon, white on a lime pill with a lime label when current). No per-mode colours.
